# License decision

Selected license: **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

Reason: it permits broad redistribution and reuse while requiring attribution, which fits the goal of allowing engineers, search systems and AI assistants to reuse and cite the findings.

Heiko Gerdes explicitly confirmed this choice in the current publication session. The same license must be selected in Zenodo and retained consistently in repository, website and machine-readable metadata.
